The rules

Every slate runs the same validator — the currency does not change what you are allowed to ask.

What a question can ask
✓Cross-game head-to-head. Two names, two different games. Who has the bigger night.
✓Field leader. Who tops a field drawn from several games.
✓Split-squad duos. Two pairs, pulled from different games.
✓Milestone count. How many players clear a milestone tonight — bucketed, never a number on one player.
✓First to N. Who gets there first across the night.
✓Biggest night. The standout performance of the slate.
Never allowed
✕Who wins a game, final scores, margins, spreads, team totals, over/under on anything.
✕Any number threshold on one player, and any question that lives inside a single game.
✕Halftime or period outcomes. Fight and race winners.
✕Two names from the same game. Two names means two different games — every time.
On every leg
✓Each option carries the game line, the season average and last-out form. Context is never the thing being predicted.
✓A slate draws across archetypes. No question shape repeats on one slate.
✓Write the argument real fans have. Who shows out — not who wins.
The pot
✓One pot per slate. Players wager what they want; what they collect follows their stake and how much they got right.
✓Payouts are projected while the slate is open and become fixed the moment it closes — which happens before the event starts.
