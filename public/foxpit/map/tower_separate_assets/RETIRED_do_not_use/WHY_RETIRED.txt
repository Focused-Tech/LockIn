tower_layer_10_stairway.png and tower_layer_20_elevator_band.png are FULL-CANVAS
1620x4500 plates with the position baked into the transparency.

That is the bug. A full-canvas plate is not a separate asset — it cannot be moved,
so compositing it at 0,0 pastes the art at whatever coordinate it happened to sit
on the source sheet. That is what put the staircase diagonally across three floors.

Use ../assets/ instead: tight-cropped pieces with an origin, independently placeable.
