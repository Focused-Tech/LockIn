THE LAW — applies to every web screen in this file
1. ONE shell. Web = this header + .wrap(1080px) + footer. Decided in ONE layout, inherited by every route. Never per-page.
2. NEVER on web: app top bar · back chevron · coin chip · bottom nav (The Floor/Board/Locksmith) · phone column · PWA banner ≥900px · boot splash on public pages.
3. Header contents and page contents share .wrap — left/right edges ALIGN.
4. Each lane routes to ITS OWN screen. Advanced → Advanced. A mis-route is a release blocker.
5. Staff lanes (Admin/Keymaster/Keyholder) render ONLY for a signed-in user with that flag, checked server-side. Anonymous sees three lanes.
6. Banned words anywhere on web: bet, betting, wager, odds, gambling, prediction, market, parlay. ("Not gambling / not sports betting" survives only inside the approved legal footer line.)
7. Event classes name the host tier and set the entries: ICON $50/$25/$10 · LEGEND (10M) $25/$15/$10 · HERO (5M) $15/$10/$5. The entry NEVER decides the band; lock-in speed does, and the multiple lands on the player's own entry. Never imply paying more improves a placing.
8. NAVIGATION. The header is PUBLIC marketing only — never lane links, never staff links, never the demo. Once a lane is chosen the header is replaced by a single lane bar naming the current lane, with movement only between creator / advanced / beginner, plus Switch lane and Sign out. Staff lanes are entered from /start by a flag-holder. The demo is reachable ONLY from inside the admin console and appears in no navigation anywhere.
9. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque display · Public Sans body · IBM Plex Mono numbers. Colors: the :root tokens in this file. Nothing else.

Event classes and entry tiers — RULED (Frank, Aug 23)
An event is classed by the HOST who runs it, and the class sets the three entries a player can pick from:
  ICON — the marquee names  ·  $50 · $25 · $10
  LEGEND — 10M  ·  $25 · $15 · $10
  HERO — 5M  ·  $15 · $10 · $5

What you put in does NOT decide your band. Lock-in speed does. Two players who lock at the same speed land in the same band; the one who entered higher simply wins a bigger number, because the multiple applies to their own entry. Paying more never buys a better placing, and paying less never costs you one. This copy must be unmissable on the entry screen — it is the whole fairness claim of the product.
Icon event Legend event Hero event

Your band, and what it pays you

Bands are set by lock-in speed among cards that got every leg right. The multiplier lands on your entry — change the tier above and every figure below re-computes.

Band ladder — APPROVED (Frank, Aug 23) · 1000× / 500× / 250× / 100× / 25× / 1.5× floor
Winners = the top 20% of the field. 100,000 entries pays 20,000; 100,000 winners needs a 500,000-entry room. The band SEATS shown are the 500k-room ladder — 1,125 players in the multiplier bands and 98,875 on the floor, which is what makes it fit.

Worked and affordable at the ruled rates:
  100k @ $10 → 20,000 winners · bands 5/10/15/20/30 · fits with $1.2K spare
  500k @ $10 → 100,000 winners · bands 5/20/40/100/960 · fits with $16K spare
  1.5M @ $25 → 300,000 winners · bands 5/25/50/150/1,000 · fits with $499K spare

Band SEAT COUNTS scale with the field; the multiples never do. RULED (Frank, Aug 23): 50,000 entries is the MINIMUM for the 1,000× top band. Below 50,000 the top band is capped at 500×, and the ladder keeps scaling down with pot size exactly as the canon place-curve does — the advertised top multiple is a function of the field, and no host may advertise a multiple their field size hasn't unlocked. The entry screen must show the CURRENT top multiple for the room's size, live.

Still open, one line each: $15 maps to no Championship division ($5/$10/$25/$50) — open a $15 division or accrue it into $10. And HERO names both an event class and the top payout band; the class keeps the name and the band reads "top band" until you name it.

The season — records, not purses
Season structure — RULED (Frank, Aug 23)
THREE SEMIFINALS, one every three months, leading into the finals. They are played for DIVISION RECORDS, not cash — the same way a sports season is played for a record and a seed. No payout during the season other than what creators earn hosting. Never render a purse, a prize or a payout on a semifinal screen.

Division leaders are decided by CATEGORY. Like the Oscars: the best performer in a category — most contests run, most paid out to their room — is that division's leader and qualifies for the finals. Every other creator keeps hosting all season; they simply are not in the finals for the big prize.

The crowns are NOT funded by the finals night. The purse ACCRUES across the whole season: a percentage of the proceeds of every competition that leads up to the finals, plus Lock In's own matching contribution. It builds over however long it takes to more than cover the target. Never describe any single event as covering the purse, and never tell a player their entry is a donation.

The ten steps

Building it

  • 1 · Pick the topic. Boss Fox takes one subject and nothing else.
  • 2 · Leg one. The Locksmith drafts it in the host's voice.
  • 3 · A bad question. The host writes one that breaks the rules.
  • 4 · The rulebook blocks it and names each violation on screen.
  • 5 · Rewritten clean, and leg three clears. Three legs, sealed.

Running it

  • 6 · Publish sealed. Nobody can see the card yet.
  • 7 · The countdown. The room fills while the card stays shut.
  • 8 · Reveal. Three questions, one clock, everybody at once.
  • 9 · Locks land. Answers in, times recorded to the millisecond.
  • 10 · Settlement. Each leg resolves against its named public source.
